    The pluggable source interface: SourceData (the SourceData refactor, R30) #

    The two-source assembly (thm_4_2, prop_8_9, prop_8_9_of) pins its candidate slot to Γ_A through the BoundaryMaps A-side fields and the _gammaA supply lemmas. This file extracts that slot into a single structure SourceData, so that a second presented source (the note's Γ_R) can be plugged in beside G_ℚ₂ with zero further refactoring: R32 builds sourceR : SourceData and instantiates thm_4_2_of_sources (GQ2/ThmFourTwo.lean).
