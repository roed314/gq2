Corollary 6.19. Complete source interface.

Call a source a topologically finitely generated profinite group \(\notnfar{GammaSrc}{\Gamma}\text{,}\) marked by four generators \(\notn{sigma}{\sigma},\notn{tau}{\tau},\notn{x0}{x_0},\notnfar{x1}{x_1}\text{,}\) whose compatible tame and maximal pro-\(2\) quotient maps assemble as in (4.2) into an epimorphism \(\notnfar{bGamma}{b_\Gamma}:\notnfar{GammaSrc}{\Gamma}\twoheadrightarrow\notnfar{bdry}{\bdry}\text{.}\) Say that a source \(\notnfar{GammaSrc}{\Gamma}\) meets the source interface if, for every finite lower quotient, every lower exact-image map, and every elementary characteristic-\(2\) coefficient module, \(\notnfar{GammaSrc}{\Gamma}\) and \(\notnfar{GQ}{\GQ}\) have:
🔗
  1. the same obstruction-space dimension and the same number of lifts when the obstruction vanishes;
    🔗
    🔗
  2. natural perfect pairings for all short exact coefficient sequences, with adjoint connecting homomorphisms;
    🔗
    🔗
  3. the same scalar cup–Bockstein form for the trivial module;
    🔗
    🔗
  4. for every nonzero invariant nonsingular quadratic form on a simple self-dual head, the same base determinant Gauss sum for the class \(\notnfar{kappaq0}{\kappa_q^0}\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
Then \(\notnfar{GA}{\GA}\) meets the source interface.
🔗
Together with the marked maximal pro-\(2\) identification in Proposition 3.10, these are the source-side inputs supplied to the target induction, and they are the only ones: after Section 5 the argument never differentiates the wild word again, so it applies verbatim to any source that meets the interface and carries the tame and pro-\(2\) data of Section 3. That is exactly what a further presentation has to supply, and Appendix C supplies it for a second one, the square-commutator presentation; the statement above is used there in this abstract form, at a source other than \(\notnfar{GA}{\GA}\text{.}\) This corollary is not by itself a determinacy statement: equality of base Gauss sums is used only after Fourier expansion. For a general pushout class
\begin{equation*} \kappa=\notnfar{kappaq0}{\kappa_q^0}+\Gamma_{\gamma_\kappa}+ \operatorname{inf}\delta_\kappa, \end{equation*}
the terms \(\gamma_\kappa\) and \(\delta_\kappa\) enter only through the scalar classes obtained by completing the square in Proposition 8.8. The corresponding signed sums are expressed as exact-image counts in central double covers of the lower target.
