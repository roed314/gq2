Lemma 2.2. Cofinality.

Every finite quotient of \(\notn{GA}{\GA}\) is an admissible marked quotient. In particular, \(\notn{WA}{\WA}\) is pro-\(2\text{.}\)

🔗

Proof.
Let \(K\triangleleft_{\mathrm{open}}F\) contain \(\notn{NA}{N_A}\text{.}\) For every \(g\notin K\text{,}\) the definition of \(\notn{NA}{N_A}\) gives an admissible quotient whose kernel does not contain \(g\text{.}\) Compactness of \(F\setminus K\) gives finitely many such kernels whose intersection is contained in \(K\text{.}\) By Lemma 2.1, this intersection is itself the kernel of an admissible quotient. Therefore \(F/K\) is a quotient of an admissible quotient, and admissibility passes to quotients. The last assertion follows because every finite image of \(\notn{WA}{\WA}\) is a \(2\)-group.
