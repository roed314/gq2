Lemma 8.4. Fourier inversion.

Let \(\Lambda\) be the character group of a finite \(\F_2\)-obstruction space and let \(o:X\to \Lambda^\vee\text{.}\) If
\begin{equation*} m_\lambda=\#\{x\in X:\langle\lambda,o(x)\rangle=0\}, \end{equation*}
then
\begin{equation} \#\{x:o(x)=0\} =\frac1{|\Lambda|}\sum_{\lambda\in \Lambda}(2m_\lambda-|X|).\tag{8.2} \end{equation}

Proof.
Use \(1_{o=0}=|\Lambda|^{-1}\sum_{\lambda\in \Lambda}(-1)^{\langle\lambda,o\rangle}\text{.}\)
