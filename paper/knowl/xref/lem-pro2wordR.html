Lemma C.8. Pro-\(2\) quotient of the square-commutator candidate.

The maximal pro-\(2\) quotient of \(\Gamma_R\) is
\begin{equation} D_R=\angles{s,x,y\;\middle|\;(x^s)^{-1}x^{-3}y^2[y,y^s]}_{\mathrm{pro}-2},\tag{C.6} \end{equation}
where \(s,x,y\) are the images of \(\notn{sigma}{\sigma},\notn{x0}{x_0},\notnfar{x1}{x_1}\text{.}\)

Proof.
In a finite \(2\)-group quotient Lemma 3.1 forces \(\notn{tau}{\tau}=1\text{,}\) and \(\notn{w}{\w}\) acts as the identity. Hence \(\sigma_2=\notn{sigma}{\sigma}\text{,}\) \(a=\notn{x0}{x_0}^{-3}\text{,}\) \(y_1=\notnfar{x1}{x_1}^{\notn{sigma}{\sigma}}\) and \(c=[\notnfar{x1}{x_1},\notnfar{x1}{x_1}^{\notn{sigma}{\sigma}}]\text{,}\) so (C.2) becomes exactly the relator of (C.6). Conversely every finite quotient of (C.6) is square-commutator admissible once \(\notn{tau}{\tau}\) is set to \(1\text{.}\)
