Lemma 6.22. Marking-preserving shear of the difference data.

Let \(a\in Z^1(\notn{Cfin}{C},\notnfar{Vhead}{V})\) and define
\begin{equation*} s_a(v,c)=(v+a(c),c). \end{equation*}
If \(\kappa=\notnfar{kappaq0}{\kappa_q^0}+\Gamma_\gamma+\operatorname{inf}\delta\text{,}\) then, in cohomology,
\begin{equation} s_a^*\kappa= \notnfar{kappaq0}{\kappa_q^0}+ \Gamma_{\gamma+\notnfar{bq}{b_q}^\flat a}+ \operatorname{inf}\bigl(\delta+ \Theta_q^0(a)+\gamma\smile a\bigr),\tag{6.63} \end{equation}
where
\begin{equation} \Theta_q^0(a)=(a,\id_C)^*\notnfar{kappaq0}{\kappa_q^0}\in Z^2(\notn{Cfin}{C},\F_2)\tag{6.64} \end{equation}
and
\begin{equation} (\gamma\smile a)(c,d)=\gamma(c)(c a(d)).\tag{6.65} \end{equation}
In particular, if \(q\ne0\text{,}\) a unique cohomology class \(a\) kills the edge \(\gamma\text{,}\) and the scalar phase then becomes \(\delta+\Theta_q^0(a)+\gamma\smile a\text{.}\)

Proof.
The cocycle identity for \(a\) is exactly the condition that \(s_a\) be an automorphism over \(\notn{Cfin}{C}\text{.}\) Substitute \(v+a(c)\) and \(w+a(d)\) into the normalized representative (6.62). The terms linear in \(v\) or \(w\) change the edge by the polar map \(\notnfar{bq}{b_q}^\flat a\text{.}\) The term containing only the base class and the graph \(a\) is \(\Theta_q^0(a)\text{.}\) The old edge also contributes the scalar term \(\gamma(c)(c a(d))\text{,}\) namely (6.65). All remaining changes are normalized coboundaries. Since \(\notnfar{bq}{b_q}^\flat\) is an isomorphism, the edge can be killed uniquely in cohomology.
🔗
The shear fixes the quotient \(\notn{Cfin}{C}\text{.}\) A marked subgroup containing \(\notnfar{Vhead}{V}\) is the full inverse image of its image in \(\notn{Cfin}{C}\text{,}\) so it is preserved. Scalar decorations kill the nontrivial simple module \(\notnfar{Vhead}{V}\text{,}\) and are therefore preserved as well.
