Lemma C.17. Stage lemma.

Let \(k\ge3\) and \(T\in S_k\text{.}\) Then
🔗
  1. reachability: \(\delta_k(T)\in\im\bar d_k\text{;}\)
    🔗
    🔗
  2. digit adjustment: the map from \(\ker\bar d_k\) to \((\mathbb{Z}/2)^2\) recording the two fresh orientation digits of the corrected triple is surjective.
    🔗
    🔗
Consequently some modification of \(T\) lifts into \(S_{k+1}\text{,}\) and \(S_k\neq\emptyset\) for every \(k\ge3\text{.}\)

Proof.
(i) By Theorem C.15, applied at the generating triple \(T\text{,}\)
\begin{equation*} \delta_k(T)=\bar d_k(w)\cdot t_p^{\alpha}t_q^{\beta}, \qquad \alpha,\beta\in\F_2, \end{equation*}
where \(t_p,t_q\) are the two power classes. For each untwisted generator \(z\) let \(D_z\) be the \(\chi\)-crossed derivation of the free pro-\(2\) group taking the value \(1\) at \(z\) and \(0\) at the other two generators. Every \(\chi\)-crossed derivation kills the relator of \(D\) (that is exactly the statement that \(\chi\) is an orientation), so \(D_z\) descends to \(D\text{.}\) Reducing \(D_z\) modulo a suitable power of \(2\text{,}\) which may be done inside the finite semidirect product \(\mathbb{Z}/2^N\rtimes(\mathbb{Z}/2^N)^\times\text{,}\) gives an additive functional \(\varphi^z\) on \(Z_k\) that kills \(\im\bar d_k\text{;}\) and the \(2\times2\) matrix formed by the two functionals on the two power classes is invertible over \(\F_2\text{,}\) by the generation clause alone. Finally the congruence clause forces \(\varphi^z(\delta_k(T))=0\text{:}\) written out, this vanishing is the orientation identity of the tested group at its own generator values, namely \(3+\notn{eta}{\eta}^{-1}=0\) for \(\notn{r0}{r_0}\) and the relation among \(S,X,Y\) of (C.8) for \(r_2\text{.}\) Hence \(\alpha=\beta=0\text{.}\)
🔗
(ii) The digit in the twisted slot is automatic. Applying \(\chi\) to the relator clause one level up, and using \(\chi(\lambda_j)\subseteq1+2^{j+1}\mathbb{Z}_2\) at \(j=k+1\text{,}\) gives a relation modulo \(2^{k+2}\) among the orientation values of the lifted triple; the corresponding identity holds exactly at the targets, since \((-1)^2\cdot1^4=1\) and \(X^{-4}Y^2=1\text{.}\) Comparing the two forces the fresh digit of the twisted slot to vanish, with no adjustment and with no data from level \(k+2\text{.}\) The remaining two digits are moved by explicit elements of \(\ker\bar d_k\text{:}\) modifications whose slots are \(2^{k-2}\)-nd powers of the canonical lifts themselves, so that the brackets they would contribute vanish because an element commutes with its own powers. The digit each such modification carries is computed by lifting the exponent from the exact valuations
\begin{equation*} v_2(\notn{eta}{\eta}-1)=v_2(X-1)=v_2(S-1)=2, \end{equation*}
which place a nonzero digit at position exactly \(k\text{.}\) Two such moves span \((\mathbb{Z}/2)^2\text{.}\)
🔗
For the last assertion, combine (i) and (ii): correct \(T\) by a modification realizing the inverse of \(\delta_k(T)\text{,}\) then adjust inside \(\ker\bar d_k\text{.}\) Generation survives because modifications lie in \(\lambda_{k-1}\subseteq\notn{Frattini}{\Phi}(Q_k)\text{.}\) The base case \(S_3\neq\emptyset\) is checked directly in \(Q_3\text{,}\) a group of order \(2^8\text{:}\) the triples \((y,sx,x)\) and \((SY,Y,\notn{Agen}{A})\) serve in the two directions.
