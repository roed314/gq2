Lemma 6.20. No square-only invariant.

Let \(\notnfar{Vhead}{V}\) be a nontrivial simple \(\F_2[\notn{Cfin}{C}]\)-module. Every nonzero element of \(H^0(\notn{Cfin}{C},\operatorname{Sym}^2\notnfar{Vhead}{V}^\vee)\) has nonzero, hence nonsingular, polar form.
🔗
Lean
🔗

Proof.
The zero-polar submodule is the Frobenius-square copy of \(\notnfar{Vhead}{V}^\vee\text{,}\) whose fixed subspace is zero. The radical of a nonzero invariant polar form is a submodule of the simple module \(\notnfar{Vhead}{V}\text{.}\)
