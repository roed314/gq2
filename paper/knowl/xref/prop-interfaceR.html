Proof.
Item (i) is the argument of Proposition 3.2 verbatim: in the quotient by the wild subgroup the elements \(\notn{x0}{x_0},\notnfar{x1}{x_1}\) vanish, and Lemma 3.1 gives \(\notn{tau}{\tau}^{\notn{w}{\w}}=1\text{,}\) so \(a=1\) and (C.2) disappears exactly as (1.6) does. Item (ii) is Proposition C.12, proved in Subsection C.4.
🔗
For the remaining items the calculations of Section 5 and Section 6 are repeated with (C.2) in place of (1.6), using the word ledgers of Appendix B, which are statements about evaluation in the finite semidirect, Heisenberg and extraspecial groups and are available for any word. The first-order calculation is the shortest of these: the evaluated wild row of the Fox Jacobian of Lemma 5.5 for (C.2) is the row of (1.6) with its two wild columns interchanged. Consequently the lifting complex has the same dimensions, and the argument of Proposition 5.15 gives items (i) and (ii) of Corollary 6.19 unchanged; the trivial-module collapse and the Stokes endpoint of Proposition 5.8 give item (iii); and the second-order expansion of Proposition 6.5, applied to (C.2), produces a base quadratic form with the zero counts of Proposition 6.9, hence item (iv).
🔗
The square-commutator relator is the shorter word at every seam, and the calculations are correspondingly shorter: it has no analogue of the auxiliary words \(h_0,d_0,z_0,g_0\) of (1.1)–(1.3), its square block is the bare \(\notnfar{x1}{x_1}^2\text{,}\) and its ramified base evaluation lands on the Wall form of Lemma 6.6 without the telescoping corrections that (1.6) requires; several auxiliary hypotheses used in the main text are correspondingly unnecessary here. In the formalization the independence of the induction from the source is made literal: the interface is a structure, the induction is proved once over an abstract instance of it, and the two sources are two instantiations.
