Proposition C.11. Demushkin classification at rank \(3\) and \(q=2\).

Let \(D\) be a Demushkin pro-\(2\) group of rank \(3\) with \(q(D)=2\text{,}\) carrying a continuous surjective character \(\chi:D\to\mathbb{Z}_2^\times\) that is an orientation of \(D\text{.}\) Then \(D\cong \notn{D0}{D_0}\) as topological groups.

Proof.
This is the instance \(n=3\text{,}\) \(q=2\) of the classification of Demushkin groups, [Lab, Thms. 4(2) and 8]. The surjectivity hypothesis pins the remaining invariant: for a Demushkin group of this shape the image of the orientation is \(\{\pm1\}\times(1+2^f\mathbb{Z}_2)\text{,}\) and surjectivity says \(f=2\text{.}\) The instance is proved directly in Subsection C.5.
