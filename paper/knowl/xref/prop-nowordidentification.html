Proposition C.6. Obstruction to a word identification.

Let \(G,H\in\{\notn{D0}{D_0},D_R\}\text{.}\)
🔗
  1. Every continuous epimorphism \(G\twoheadrightarrow H\) is an isomorphism.
    🔗
    🔗
  2. Every continuous isomorphism intertwines the canonical orientations of the two groups.
    🔗
    🔗
  3. There is no triple of words in the generators of \(\notn{D0}{D_0}\) whose images generate \(D_R\) and satisfy its relation, and none in the generators of \(D_R\) for \(\notn{D0}{D_0}\text{.}\) The same holds for words in the four generators of \(\notnfar{GA}{\GA}\) or of \(\Gamma_R\text{.}\)
    🔗
    🔗
🔗

Proof.
Both groups have \(\dim_{\F_2}H^1(-,\F_2)=3\) and \(\dim_{\F_2}H^2(-,\F_2)=1\text{.}\) Let \(f:G\twoheadrightarrow H\) be continuous with \(K=\ker f\neq1\text{.}\) Inflation is injective on \(H^1\text{,}\) hence bijective by equality of dimensions, so the restriction \(H^1(G)\to H^1(K)^G\) is zero. The five-term exact sequence then makes transgression injective on \(H^1(K)^G\text{,}\) which is nonzero because \(K\) is a nontrivial pro-\(2\) group; so transgression is onto \(H^2(H)\cong\F_2\text{,}\) and exactness makes inflation \(H^2(H)\to H^2(G)\) vanish. But the cup product on \(H^1(G)\) is the inflation of the cup product on \(H^1(H)\text{,}\) and would therefore be zero, contradicting the nondegeneracy recorded in Lemma C.9 and in Proposition 1.1. Hence \(K=1\text{,}\) which is (i). Assertion (ii) holds because the dualizing module of a Demushkin group is unique and \(\Aut(\mathbb{Z}_2)\) is abelian, so the orientation character is canonical.
🔗
For (iii), a word triple as described would define an epimorphism, hence by (i) an isomorphism, hence by (ii) one intertwining the orientations. By Proposition C.10 the orientation of \(D_R\) takes the values \(S,X,Y\) on its generators; these are units of the cubic field \(K=\mathbb{Q}(X)\) of discriminant \(-59\text{,}\) with
\begin{equation*} N_{K/\mathbb{Q}}(X)=-1, \qquad N_{K/\mathbb{Q}}(S)=1/4, \qquad N_{K/\mathbb{Q}}(Y)=-1, \end{equation*}
so the orientation value of any word in the generators of \(D_R\) has norm in \(\pm4^{\mathbb{Z}}\text{.}\) An isomorphism \(\notn{D0}{D_0}\to D_R\) would have to carry \(Y\) to an element of orientation value \(\notn{eta}{\eta}=(-3)^{-1}\text{,}\) of norm \(-1/27\text{;}\) impossible. In the other direction the orientation of \(\notn{D0}{D_0}\) takes the rational values \(-1,1,\notn{eta}{\eta}\) on its generators, so every word value is rational, whereas \(X\) is a cubic irrational. Finally, in any pro-\(2\) quotient the tame generator dies and exponentiation by \(\notn{w}{\w}\) is the identity, so words in the four generators factor through words in the three pro-\(2\) generators and the same obstruction applies.
