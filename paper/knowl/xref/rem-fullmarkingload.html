Remark 3.15. Why the full marking is load-bearing.

The construction
\begin{equation*} \notn{bdry}{\bdry}=\notnfar{TA}{\TA}\times_{\mathbb{Z}_2}\notn{Pi}{\Pi} \end{equation*}
uses the full marked isomorphism \((\notn{Pi}{\Pi},\notn{nu2}{\nu_2})\cong(G_{\Qtwo}(2),\notn{nuur}{\nu_{\mathrm{ur}}})\text{.}\) An unmarked Demushkin classification identifies only the abstract pro-\(2\) group; it does not fix the terminal comparison after passage to all finite quotients \(C_{2^m}\) of the unramified coordinate.
