Remark 3.13. The integral marking is not detected by the mod-\(2\) cup form.

The more familiar unmarked Labute relator
\begin{equation*} \notn{x0}{x_0}^2\notnfar{x1}{x_1}^4[\notnfar{x1}{x_1},\notn{sigma}{\sigma}] \end{equation*}
has the same quadratic initial form \(b^2+ac+ca\) as (3.17). It does not, however, admit the marking
\begin{equation*} \nu(\notn{sigma}{\sigma})=1, \qquad \nu(\notn{x0}{x_0})=\nu(\notnfar{x1}{x_1})=0 \end{equation*}
required by the tame boundary. In those Labute variables the canonical orientation has values
\begin{equation*} \chi(\notn{x0}{x_0})=-1, \qquad \chi(\notnfar{x1}{x_1})=1, \qquad \chi(\notn{sigma}{\sigma})=(-3)^{-1}. \end{equation*}
The abelianization is \(\mathbb{Z}_2^2\oplus C_2\text{,}\) and \(\bar x_1\) has infinite order. The asserted marking would nevertheless put \(\notnfar{x1}{x_1}\) in \(\ker\nu\cap\ker\chi\text{,}\) contradicting the injectivity of \((\notn{nuur}{\nu_{\mathrm{ur}}},\chi)\) on \(\notn{Dpro2}{D}^{\mathrm{ab}}\text{.}\) Thus agreement of the maximal pro-\(2\) quotient as an unmarked Demushkin group is insufficient. The Nielsen transformation (3.16) replaces that relator by (3.17). It has the same mod-\(2\) two-jet; its full unramified marking follows from Proposition 1.1.
