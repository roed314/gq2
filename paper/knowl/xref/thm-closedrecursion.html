Theorem 8.17. Closed exact-image recursion.

Let \(\notnfar{calY}{\mathcal Y}=(\notnfar{Ytarget}{Y},\notnfar{LY}{L_Y},\notnfar{piY}{\pi_Y},\notnfar{thetaY}{\theta_Y})\) be a boundary-framed marked target (Definition 4.1) and suppose \(\notnfar{LY}{L_Y}\) has a non-scalar simple head, with the minimal-block tower (7.1) chosen as above. When \(\notnfar{Rcent}{R}\ne1\text{,}\) the recursion uses, for each nonzero scalar functional \(\lambda\in\notnfar{calX}{\mathcal X_R}\text{,}\) the descended nonsingular \(C\)-invariant form \(\ol q_\lambda\) on \(V\) of Proposition 7.4, its fixed equivariant base determinant class \(\kappa^0_{\ol q_\lambda}\) of Lemma 6.3, and the radical data \(T\) with its edge class \([\bar\varepsilon]\) of Lemma 8.6. The exact-image count \(\notnfar{eGamma}{e_\Gamma}(\notnfar{Ytarget}{Y})\) is obtained from (8.13) and, when \(\notnfar{Rcent}{R}\ne1\text{,}\) from (8.15)–(8.22). Every exact-image count on the right-hand sides belongs to a boundary-framed target with strictly smaller marked \(2\)-kernel. Hence the count is determined by the source interface of Corollary 6.19 and the exact-image counts for smaller targets.

Proof.
The elementary stage is Lemma 8.9. If \(\notnfar{Rcent}{R}=1\text{,}\) then \(\notnfar{Bmid}{B}=\notnfar{Ytarget}{Y}\text{.}\) Otherwise Lemma 8.10, Proposition 8.11 reduce the final \(\notnfar{Rcent}{R}\)-obstruction to its scalar pushouts. Proper images are removed by Lemma 8.12. The unrestricted scalar count is given by Proposition 8.13 when the radical edge is nonzero and by Proposition 8.14 when it vanishes. The signed scalar phases in the latter formula are exact-image counts by Lemma 8.15. Strict decrease is Lemma 8.16.
