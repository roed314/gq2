Theorem C.3. Replacement theorem.

\(\Gamma_R\cong\notnfar{GQ}{\GQ}\text{.}\) Equivalently,
\begin{equation} |\Sur(\Gamma_R,G)|=|\Sur(\notnfar{GQ}{\GQ},G)|\tag{C.4} \end{equation}
for every finite group \(G\text{;}\) equivalently again, \(\mathrm{ac}_R(G)=|\Sur(\notnfar{GQ}{\GQ},G)|\) for every finite group \(G\text{.}\) Consequently the two candidates present the same group, and their marking counts agree: \(\mathrm{ac}_R(G)=\mathrm{ac}(G)\) for every finite \(G\text{.}\)

Proof.
By Proposition C.5 the source interface of Corollary 6.19 holds for \(\Gamma_R\text{,}\) so Theorem 4.2 and its proof apply with \(\Gamma_R\) in place of \(\notnfar{GA}{\GA}\text{.}\) Summing over tame boundary frames as in Lemma 10.1 gives (C.4). Since \(\Gamma_R\) is topologically generated by four elements, Lemma 2.5 converts (C.4) into an isomorphism. The last assertion combines (C.4) with (10.1) and Proposition 2.3.
